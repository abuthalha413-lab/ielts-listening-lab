LISTENING STRATEGY

Quick IELTS Listening tips

Before listening

Read the questions, underline key words, and predict the type of answer.

While listening

Follow the question order. If you miss one answer, move on instead of getting stuck.

Spelling

Check spelling, plurals, numbers, dates, and word limits carefully.

After the test

Review every wrong answer and identify whether the problem was vocabulary, attention, spelling, or prediction.
